Xinghe Technology Expense Policy

1. Transportation
Local business travel may be reimbursed for public transportation. Ride-hailing expenses should generally not exceed 100 CNY per trip. Higher amounts require an explanation in the expense claim and approval from the direct manager.

2. Meals
The daily travel meal allowance is 30 CNY for breakfast, 50 CNY for lunch, and 50 CNY for dinner. Meal allowances and actual meal invoices cannot be reimbursed for the same expense.

3. Submission Deadline
Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days after the expense occurs. Claims submitted later require an explanation and approval from the department head.

4. Required Materials
Expense claims must include valid invoices, payment records, and expense details. Expenses generally cannot be reimbursed without an invoice.

5. Approval Process
After submission, the direct manager reviews the claim first, followed by Finance. Single expenses above 5,000 CNY also require approval from the General Manager.
