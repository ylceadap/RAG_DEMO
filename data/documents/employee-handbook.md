# Xinghe Technology Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

The standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, with lunch from 12:00 to 13:00. Employees must record working hours through the company attendance system.

## Onboarding Process

New employees must complete four steps: submit identification and bank information, attend onboarding training, receive a computer and work accounts, and confirm probation goals with their direct manager. After all materials are submitted, Human Resources usually activates the accounts within three business days.

## Leave Rules

Leave requests must be submitted in the system at least one business day in advance. More than three consecutive days of leave require approval from both the direct manager and the department head. In emergencies, employees may notify their manager through instant messaging first and submit the system request later.

## Annual Leave

After completing one year of service, employees receive ten paid annual-leave days per year. Annual leave should be used within the same year. If business needs prevent its use, the department head must approve the carryover before year-end.
